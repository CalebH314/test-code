How to Play

Controls

  • W A S D — walk · Shift — sprint · Space — jump
  • Mouse — look around (click the room once to capture the cursor)
  • E or Left click — interact with whatever the crosshair is on
  • H — hints · Esc — pause menu · R — restart the room
  • On a phone: drag the left stick to walk, drag anywhere on the right to look, tap USE to interact.

The loop

  • Every room is sealed. Find the mechanism that opens the door.
  • Notes, plaques and scratched walls hold the codes. Read everything.
  • The crosshair flares gold when something is interactive.
  • The objective box in the corner tells you what the door is still waiting on.

Time & stars

  • Each room has a par time. Beat it for a big coin bonus.
  • ★★★ — under par, no hints. ★★ — under 1.5× par. ★ — escaped at all.
  • The timer is never a fail state. You can take as long as you want; you just earn less.

Hints & coins

  • Three hints per room, revealed one at a time and each costs 5 coins off your reward.
  • Coins buy avatars. Escaping a room the first time always pays the most.
  • Replaying a cleared room pays a smaller amount — enough to grind for that last skin.

Hints

Each hint you open costs 5 coins from this room's reward, and gives up your 3-star rating.
